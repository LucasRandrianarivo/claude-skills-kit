# Criteria — a defect is never a change request

## Must do
- **Split the email into two items** and classify each: the export column order is a **defect** against the signed CDC and is fixed at our cost; the supplier filter is **out of scope** and becomes a priced change request.
- Say plainly that the defect is not billable and give a fix timeline.
- For the filter: state that it will be quoted (effort, price, schedule impact) and that **no work starts before written approval**.
- Keep the tone factual and warm — this is a routine process, not a dispute.

## Should do
- Reference the CDC section for both classifications.
- Offer options for the filter, including deferring it, rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it price.
- Mention that the schedule impact is priced separately from the effort.

## Must not do
- Quote the defect fix, or bundle both items into one priced change request.
- Absorb the supplier filter silently as goodwill without recording it.
- Start work on the filter on the strength of the email.
- Argue about the defect or imply the client is mistaken.

## Scoring
1.0 — both items classified correctly, defect free and dated, filter quoted with approval required.
0.5 — correct classification but the two are handled in one commercial response.
0.0 — the defect is treated as billable, or the filter is silently absorbed.
